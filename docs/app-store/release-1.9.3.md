# Wendao AI 1.9.3 (27)

## Change

Signed-in non-members can now ask 3 free Wendao AI questions each UTC day, while members remain unlimited. The conversation shows the remaining free count and a clear membership path. Share link now sends the canonical URL as the only shared content item, fixing iOS system Copy choosing descriptive text instead of the link. Image sharing remains PNG-only.

The former 30-day introductory campaign and the lifetime product's temporary-price reversion were removed in App Store Connect. Fixed standard prices begin on 2026-09-23: monthly US$9.99 / mainland China CNY19.90, annual US$89.99 / mainland China CNY159.00, and lifetime reading US$9.99 / mainland China CNY19.90. Apple does not offer a CNY159.90 price point; CNY159.00 is the closest supported tier. Other storefronts use Apple's localized prices from the USD base. Existing subscribers already inside an introductory period retain the terms Apple previously granted.

## What's New — zh-Hans

登录后每天可免费问道 3 条，会员仍可不限次数对话。会员改为长期固定价格，不再使用限时首购优惠。同时修复“分享链接”，系统“拷贝”会正确复制章节或文章链接。

## What's New — en-US

Sign in for 3 free Wendao AI questions each day; members remain unlimited. Membership now uses fixed standard pricing with no limited-time introductory offer. Share Link now copies the correct chapter or Tao in Everyday Life article URL.
