Wendao AI 1.9.8 (33) combines bilingual Daodejing reading and chapter-grounded reflection. Today's chapter is free; readers may keep 10 additional chapters free. Contents and search do not consume a choice.

This update improves night-mode readability, adds search/theme/chapter filters and thumbnail browsing for all 81 bilingual comics, and offers received-text or silk-manuscript Contents ordering. Reading and membership now has a separate entry that presents benefits, plans and StoreKit prices before sign-in. The selected monthly, annual or lifetime product remains selected through sign-in. Free AI usage and membership status refresh from the server when returning to the app and at the daily reset.

DOWNLOAD SIZE: All 81 chapter texts and the core reader remain bundled. Comic previews and enlarged reading use on-demand WebP from https://wendao.wonderelian.com. Recent previews are cached locally, limited to 12 images and 16 MiB, with a Clear Comic Cache control in More. Uncached comics need a network connection. Original PNG is fetched only when saving or sharing, with timeout, cancel and retry controls. Image sharing sends the image file. Saving uses Photos add-only permission; a denied permission shows an Open Settings action. Reading access, StoreKit products and prices are unchanged.

Unlock All Chapters Forever grants reading only. Purchases use StoreKit and localized prices. No web payment or internal test membership override is present in this Release build.
