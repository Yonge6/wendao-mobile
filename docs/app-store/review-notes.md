# App Review Notes · Version 1.9.2 (24)

Wendao AI combines bilingual Daodejing reading and chapter-grounded reflection. Today's chapter is always free; readers may deliberately keep 10 additional chapters free on the device. Contents and search do not consume a choice. All 81 chapters are bundled for offline reading, subject to reading access.

Retained from approved 1.9: Tao in everyday life contains 87 authored, AI-assisted and editorially reviewed essays in Chinese covering all 81 chapters. They are bundled in the app and available in More and as section 04 after each chapter's insights. English navigation explicitly labels these essays as Chinese. Each essay includes a source quotation, everyday reflection and practice. It is contemporary commentary, not a new translation of the classic. Share this layer opens a complete essay poster with its source, practice and canonical article QR. The share panel supports image saving and copying an article link. These are reading links, not purchase or external checkout links.

NEW IN 1.9.2: Build 24 reads introductory offer terms and eligibility from StoreKit. Eligible users see the localized discounted price, full offer period and standard renewal price together. Others see standard pricing. Prices can be refreshed. Apple determines eligibility and confirms the final purchase; no eligibility override or test membership is included. Prior night-mode and PNG-only sharing fixes are retained.

Suggested review:
1. Read today's free chapter, then scroll past section 03 to section 04 and open Share this layer. Save its image to Photos (system permission required on first use) or copy its article link. Close the panel and confirm the reading position remains.
2. Open More > Tao in everyday life, select an essay and return to its related chapter. Switch Chinese/English; the essays remain clearly labeled Chinese.
3. On the membership screen, inspect eligible introductory terms and renewal price, then reload prices. An annual offer covers the full first year; monthly covers the first month. Test ineligible users to confirm standard pricing.
4. Sign in with Apple or Google to test AI and StoreKit in Apple's sandbox. No separate credentials are needed for federated sign-in. Test Restore Purchases and recent conversations. On a wider screen, opening AI shows reading and conversation side by side; drafts and active responses survive resizing.

AI requires sign-in and an active monthly or annual App Store subscription, with no free trial. Either subscription unlocks all chapters and unlimited AI questions, subject to rate and abuse safeguards. The non-consumable Unlock All Chapters Forever purchase grants reading only, not AI, memory or weekly reflection. Purchases use StoreKit and localized prices. No external checkout or web-payment entry is shown in iOS. There are no internal test memberships in this Release build.

Life Manual is optional. Voluntary birth details generate a chart summary and reflective interpretation; no BodyGraph is shown. AI uses the current chapter, dialogue and user-controlled memory. DeepSeek processes responses in China, disclosed before sign-in. Raw birth details and payment information are not sent to the model. Users can inspect, edit, pause or delete memories, export data and delete their account. This supports reflection, not medical, psychological, legal, financial or deterministic advice.

This update retains the approved 1.8 AI connectivity repair, native streaming, direct Photos save, clear copy/save feedback, daily local notifications and small/medium widgets. Only standard HTTPS is used; ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption is false. Existing worldwide availability and Lifestyle category are unchanged. The app includes Daodejing content and does not claim a China mainland internet publishing permit. Prior Guideline 2.1 correspondence requested publication authorization; subsequent versions were approved.
