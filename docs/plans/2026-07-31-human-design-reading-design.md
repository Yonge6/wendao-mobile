# Human Design reading inside Wendao

## Goal

The Life Manual should explain the calculated result instead of stopping at five labels. It should keep the reader inside Wendao, never render a BodyGraph, and never present Human Design as a deterministic verdict.

## Information hierarchy

1. The result card keeps the five scannable facts: type, strategy, authority, profile, and definition.
2. A four-part foundational reading follows: core advantage, talent combination, life theme, and best expression.
3. A single `查看详细解读 / Read the detailed guide` button opens a second drawer view with twelve continuous reading themes.
4. The detailed view uses the verified snapshot's type, strategy, authority, profile, definition, incarnation cross, personality/design Sun and Earth gates, and variable activations.

## Navigation

The home drawer's Life Manual status card is the only entry to birth details and the reading. The duplicate Life Manual row is removed from the navigation list. Night reading, About, and Feedback remain as utilities.

## Content boundary

The reading structure follows the companion Human Design project's foundational and detailed-reading modules, adapted to Wendao's quieter editorial voice. It excludes the diagram and celebrity matching. Every reading ends with a reflection-only disclaimer.

## Acceptance

- A verified result displays four meaningful foundational sections above a detailed-reading button.
- The detailed view contains twelve personalized sections and returns to the profile form.
- Chinese and English both work.
- Existing snapshots without activation detail still receive a safe, useful fallback reading.
- At mobile width, the result and detailed view scroll without overlap or horizontal overflow.
