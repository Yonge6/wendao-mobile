# Search and life-manual sharing

## Reader requests

Search returned many chapter titles without enough evidence to choose one. The life-manual sharing tab was disabled before birth details were entered, hiding both its value and its entry point.

## Decisions

- Preserve the ordinary directory and its silk order. In a nonempty search, rank exact chapter/title matches ahead of text, translation, guidance and editorial references.
- Display the actual matching field, a short contextual excerpt and safely rendered highlighting. Match within fields, not across concatenated chapter data. Reuse a lazy per-chapter index to avoid startup work.
- Selecting a match opens its language and passage where rendered, otherwise its reading section. Preserve all reading gates and deliberate free-chapter choices; a pending destination survives a deliberate unlock.
- Keep all four sharing tabs enabled. Before a verified chart exists, the manual tab displays an explanation of its reflective value and limitations, anonymous-sharing privacy, and one birth-form action. Do not render a placeholder manual poster or expose export controls in this state.
- Reuse paper, teal and gold tokens and existing typefaces. Preserve the fixed tabs/actions with a bounded scrolling middle, including small screens and English copy.

## Verification

Unit coverage: ranking, exact numeric lookup, bilingual and reference coverage, field boundaries, literal highlighting and late excerpts. Browser coverage: passage positioning, locked search selection, free-choice preservation, clearing search, bilingual manual invitation, birth-form navigation, existing anonymous posters and small-screen layouts. Re-run the existing product and runtime suites.
