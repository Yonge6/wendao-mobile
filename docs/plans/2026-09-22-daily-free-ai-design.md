# Daily free Wendao AI

Signed-in readers without an active membership may ask three Wendao AI questions per UTC day. Active and grace-period members remain unlimited. The server is the authority: it reserves a question atomically before model work, replays an already completed request without another charge, releases failed or abandoned reservations, and keeps the existing concurrency and burst safeguards.

The conversation remains available to non-members instead of being replaced by the subscription screen. It shows the free daily allowance and the current remaining count, then offers membership for unlimited questions. When the third free question has been used, the composer is disabled and the membership action becomes prominent. Weekly reflection remains a membership feature.
