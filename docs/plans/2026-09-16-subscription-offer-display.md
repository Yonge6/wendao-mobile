# Subscription offer display

Read offer eligibility and localized terms from StoreKit with the current product response. Pass structured optional metadata through the existing Capacitor bridge. A pure formatter rejects missing or unknown terms and only advertises eligible offers. Show the discounted period and standard renewal price together; Apple confirms the final transaction. Preserve purchase, receipt verification, entitlements and the user's installed production package.

Validation covers eligible/ineligible/missing/invalid metadata and pay-as-you-go duration, followed by the existing suite, native build, archive and App Store submission. Production checkout remains a separate verification gate.
