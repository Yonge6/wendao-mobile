# Chapter life-manual personalization

## Purpose

`你的人生说明书 / Your life manual` is a chapter-specific reflective reading for someone with a verified Human Design chart. It must not repeat, paraphrase, or embed `今日一练 / A practice for today`. The practice remains a separate, low-burden action module below the life-manual paragraph.

## Composition

The personalized paragraph combines five inputs: the chapter's distinct `对我们的启发 / What this teaches us` opening, the reader's type, strategy, authority, and profile. Type describes how energy or recognition tends to meet the chapter. Strategy explains how to enter the situation. Authority explains how clarity is recognized. Profile adds a boundary or learning reminder. The result ends by stating that it is a reflective lens rather than a decision made for the reader.

All 81 chapters use their own existing bilingual inspiration as the chapter signal. Human Design mechanics use explicit bilingual mappings rather than substituting labels into one generic paragraph. No birth data, name, chart hash, or deterministic claim enters the chapter paragraph or its anonymous share card.

## Fallback and validation

The module stays hidden until a verified chart with `chartHash` exists. Unknown chart labels receive a neutral, non-deterministic fallback while known types, strategies, authorities, and profiles use specific guidance. Regression tests assert that a verified chapter reading contains the chapter insight and all four Human Design dimensions, excludes the chapter action, keeps the separate practice unchanged, and remains complete in Chinese and English.
