# 81-chapter everyday-life comic rollout

The approved chapter 32 format is expanded to all 81 chapters: a complete humorous 12-panel everyday story, one distinct Style Atlas style per chapter, and an optional reflection explaining the actual images. On 2026-10-01 the user changed the image-generation setting from Instant to **High** in ego ChatGPT. The initial Instant drafts for chapters 1–4 were replaced with new High generations before use.

Each new comic has two 1024×1536 pages, followed by a 1024×264 footer with the original supplied brand combination on the left and a QR to the canonical story on the right. The complete 1024×3336 PNG is shared/saved; a WebP is used for reading. The exact supplied logo is composited, never redrawn. No QR caption or panel-count caption is added.

Chapter pages with a comic show that comic as their main everyday-life content. Secondary essays remain in the More archive and at their existing canonical URLs. All text and image metadata still come from `growth/copy.json`. Reflections discuss visible characters, props, humor, turning points, endings and the limited relationship to the chapter. Existing checked quotations are retained unchanged.

## First batch

Chapters 1–8 plus the existing chapter 32 are integrated. Styles: Ligne Claire, Bauhaus, Pop Art Poster, Paper Collage, Minimalism, Nordic Folk Art, Risograph and Chinese Ink Painting. Every new page was visually reviewed. All nine complete-poster QRs decode to their exact canonical article.

Production scripts, original generation pages, prompts, provenance, the 81-style assignment, review drafts and resumable browser state are under `work/life-comics-81/`. Drafts are never treated as published content.

## Second batch
